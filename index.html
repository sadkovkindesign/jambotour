Yes, most international travelers require a tourist visa to enter Tanzania. You can easily apply for an Electronic Visa (e-Visa) online through the official Tanzanian immigration website before your trip, or obtain a visa on arrival at major airports for approximately $50 USD ($100 USD for US citizens).

Tanzania is a year-round destination. Depending on whether you want to witness the Great Migration or enjoy the best weather on Zanzibar, we will help you choose the perfect dates. Generally, the dry season from June to October offers the best wildlife viewing as animals gather around water sources.

We recommend lightweight, breathable clothing in neutral tones (khaki, beige, olive green). Avoid dark blue and black as they attract tsetse flies. Essential items include a warm jacket for chilly early morning drives, a wide-brimmed hat, sunglasses, high-SPF sunscreen, effective insect repellent, and a good pair of binoculars.
